Why Marquee

Showing beats explaining. A ten second clip of the bug, the chart or the new button says more than three paragraphs in a chat.

Marquee keeps that clip one keystroke away. It waits in the tray, starts on a single shortcut and puts every recording in a folder you choose, already named and ready to send.

How it works

Three acts. No intermission.

  1. CtrlAltR

    Act I

    Press the shortcut

    One shortcut starts and stops a recording from anywhere. The area picker opens with your last area already drawn, so a repeat take is shortcut, then Enter.

  2. Free16:94:31:19:16 1920 × 1080

    Act II

    Draw the area

    Drag out any area, snap it to 16:9, 4:3, 1:1 or 9:16, or pick an exact 720p or 1080p frame. The size is shown in real pixels.

  3. Saved to Videos\Marquee Copy file

    Act III

    Stop and send

    A second or two later the MP4 is in your folder. Copy the file straight into a chat, or upload it to Yandex Disk and copy the link.

Studio

When it needs polish,
open Studio.

Turn the camera on or make the cursor editable, and the recording keeps its tracks for Studio. Frame it, cut it and export it from one window.

Device frames at real proportions

Phone, tablet, laptop or browser window, in graphite, silver, black or white. A device never stretches to fit the video: you scale it, turn it, and choose fill or fit.

Cut it like an editor

Split with T, remove with Delete, and the gap closes. A removed piece stays on the track, so Restore brings it back.

Captions written on your computer

The fine print, in large type

Every clip lands in a folder you own. Nothing leaves your computer unless you ask, and nothing is locked behind a paywall.

Free, for real

Not a trial. Not a tier.

Marquee has no paid plan, no watermark and no sign in. It is MIT licensed, so you can use it at work, change it and ship your own build.

FAQ

Questions from the front row

Is Marquee really free?

Yes. There is no paid plan and nothing is locked. The MIT license allows personal and commercial use, changes and redistribution.

Which systems does it run on?

Windows, macOS (Apple Silicon and Intel) and Linux, as a .deb, a .pacman package or an AppImage. Every installer is on the releases page.

Why does my computer warn me on the first launch?

The installers are not code signed yet. On Windows, choose More info, then Run anyway. On macOS, the install notes have a one line command that clears the quarantine flag.

Why does the installer say OpenScreen?

Marquee is the new name of OpenScreen Creator Edition. The releases on GitHub still carry the old name for now.

Does it upload my recordings?

No. Every clip is saved to your own folder. A link is made only when you press Copy link, which uploads that one file to your Yandex Disk. Sharing never deletes the clip from your folder.

Can it record my camera and my voice?

Yes. Microphone and system audio are toggles on the recorder, and window or screen recordings can include the camera as picture in picture. Area clips always include the cursor and leave the camera out.

How do captions work without the cloud?

Whisper runs on your computer. The speech model downloads once, the first time you transcribe (about 560 MB for the GPU model, 250 MB for the processor one), and works offline after that.

Can I edit a video I did not record with Marquee?

Yes. Studio opens MP4, MOV, WebM, MKV and AVI files, from the title bar, by dropping a file onto the editor, or from the recorder menu.

Is it stable?

It is young software, so expect rough edges. If something breaks, an issue on GitHub helps the most.
