**OpenScreen Creator Edition 1.10.0** — saving is instant, the timeline works like other editors, and system audio stays in sync.

**Коротко по-русски.** Клип сохраняется сразу после остановки, сколько бы он ни длился. Таймлайн как в CapCut и Final Cut: удалили кусок — остальное сдвинулось. Исправлен звук в записи без микрофона, тень дока, удаление клипа в корзину; «Copy file» теперь главная кнопка, а загрузка на Яндекс Диск сообщает о себе.

## What's new

- **Clips are saved the moment you stop (Windows).** The area is cropped and scaled to the chosen quality on the GPU while it records, so Stop only moves the finished file into your folder. A 4.5 s clip took about 2 s to save and a minute about half a minute; now any length takes under a second.
- **A timeline like other editors.** Removing a piece of the clip closes the gap: what follows moves up and the video gets shorter, on the timeline and in the player. A scissors mark where a part was cut out puts it back. **Ctrl+B** (Cmd+B) splits the clip and **Delete** removes the selected piece.
- **Copy file comes first.** It is the card's main button; Copy link is the second. When an upload to Yandex Disk finishes, the dock says so and the card shows the link.

## Fixes

- **System audio without a microphone no longer drifts.** Windows sends no system audio while nothing plays, so quiet stretches fell out of the recording and sounds came early. The recorder now keeps the output device active while it records.
- **Move to trash** waits out short-lived locks on a fresh clip, and a failed upload no longer keeps the file open.
- **The dock's shadow** is no longer cut off when the dock is dragged away from the bottom of the screen.
